Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 27, 2025

Flicklet ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web service (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use our Service, we collect:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, username (optional), and profile photo
  • Content Data: Your watchlists (Currently Watching, Want to Watch, Watched), ratings, notes, and tags for TV shows and movies
  • Episode Progress: Information about which episodes you have watched
  • Preferences: Theme preferences (light/dark mode), notification settings, and layout preferences
  • Game Statistics: Scores and statistics from FlickWord and Daily Trivia games
  • Location Data (Optional): City/region information you provide for theater showtimes (we do not collect GPS coordinates)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our Service, we automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the Service
  • Error Logs: Technical information when errors occur (anonymized)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and browser information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Service
  • Sync your data across devices when you sign in
  • Send you notifications about upcoming episodes and new content (with your permission)
  • Personalize your experience, including recommendations based on your preferences
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns to improve our Service
  • Detect and prevent technical issues and security threats

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate our Service:

  • Firebase (Google): For authentication, data storage (Firestore), and push notifications (Cloud Messaging)
  • Sentry: For error tracking and performance monitoring (anonymized data only)
  • SendGrid: For email delivery of notifications (Pro users only)
  • The Movie Database (TMDB): For TV show and movie metadata and images

These service providers are bound by contractual obligations to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in:

  • Firebase Firestore: Cloud database for syncing data across devices
  • Local Storage: Browser/device storage for offline access and performance

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can access your data through the Service at any time
  • Correction: You can update your information through your account settings
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and data by contacting us (see Contact section below)
  • Opt-Out: You can disable push notifications and email notifications in your account settings
  • Data Portability: You can export your watchlist data through the Service

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country. By using our Service, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the Service. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
